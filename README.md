## ✨ Overview

Montra is an intelligent personal finance assistant designed to help people understand, track, and improve their financial habits using artificial intelligence.

Instead of manually managing expenses, Montra uses AI to analyze spending patterns, extract information from receipts, and provide personalized financial insights.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Many people struggle with understanding where their money goes.

Common problems:

- Manual expense tracking is boring and time-consuming
- People don't notice unhealthy spending habits
- Financial decisions are often made without enough information

---

## 💡 The Solution

Montra turns everyday spending into meaningful insights.

With AI-powered features, users can:

- Automatically track expenses
- Scan and understand receipts
- Analyze spending habits
- Receive personalized financial recommendations

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📸 AI Receipt Scanner
Automatically extract information from receipts:

- Store name
- Date
- Total amount
- Purchased items
- Expense category


### 🤖 AI Financial Assistant

Ask questions about your finances:

> "Why did I spend more this month?"

> "How can I save more money?"

Montra provides personalized answers based on your spending behavior.


### 📊 Smart Analytics

Visualize:

- Spending trends
- Categories
- Monthly changes
- Financial habits


### 🔔 Intelligent Insights

Montra detects patterns and provides useful suggestions:

- Unusual spending
- Subscription costs
- Saving opportunities
